## Project Overview

This project analyses employment statistics in the United States, focusing on three technology-related occupations:

- Software Developers
- Data Scientists
- Computer and Information Research Scientists

The analysis uses employment data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) and focuses primarily on May 2023 employment statistics.

The project explores employment levels, salary distributions, and employment trends using Python, Pandas and Matplotlib.

The visualisations developed in this project include:

1. Total employment comparison across technology roles
2. Employment share by technology role
3. Employment and mean annual wage comparison
4. State-level wage distribution
5. Employment trends between 2022 and 2023

The project also includes an additional analysis comparing May 2022 and May 2023 employment data to investigate changes in employment over time.
